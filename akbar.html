Akbar - Important Facts

  • Full Name: Jalal-ud-din Muhammad Akbar
  • Born: 15 October 1542
  • Father: Humayun
  • Mother: Hamida Banu Begum
  • Reign: 1556–1605
  • Dynasty: Mughal Dynasty
  • Capital: Agra; later Fatehpur Sikri

1. Early Life

Akbar was born on 15 October 1542 at Umarkot in Sindh. He was the son of Mughal emperor Humayun and Hamida Banu Begum.

Akbar became emperor in 1556 after the death of Humayun. At that time, Akbar was only about 14 years old.

2. Second Battle of Panipat

The Second Battle of Panipat was fought in 1556.

  • Akbar's army fought against Hemu.
  • Akbar's forces won the battle.
  • The victory helped establish Mughal power in northern India.
  • Bairam Khan played an important role in guiding the young Akbar.
Exam Point: Second Battle of Panipat - 1556 - Akbar vs Hemu.

3. Expansion of the Mughal Empire

Akbar expanded the Mughal Empire through military campaigns, diplomacy and alliances.

  • Gujarat was conquered in 1572.
  • Bengal was brought under Mughal control.
  • Several Rajput kingdoms accepted Mughal authority.
  • Akbar also expanded towards the Deccan.

4. Rajput Policy

Akbar followed a policy of friendship and alliance with many Rajput rulers.

  • He established matrimonial alliances with some Rajput families.
  • Rajput nobles were given important positions in the Mughal administration.
  • Many Rajputs became important military commanders.
  • However, Akbar also fought Rajput states that resisted Mughal authority.
Important: Maharana Pratap of Mewar resisted Mughal expansion.

5. Religious Policy

Akbar is known for his relatively tolerant religious policy.

  • Pilgrimage tax was abolished in 1563.
  • Jizya tax was abolished in 1564.
  • He established the Ibadat Khana at Fatehpur Sikri.
  • He promoted the idea of Sulh-i-Kul, meaning universal peace.

6. Din-i-Ilahi

Akbar introduced Din-i-Ilahi in 1582. It was an ethical and spiritual order influenced by ideas from different religious traditions.

Din-i-Ilahi had very few followers and did not become a mass religion.

7. Administration

Mansabdari System

Akbar developed the Mansabdari system to organize civil and military officials. Mansabdars were given ranks and responsibilities.

Revenue Administration

Raja Todar Mal played an important role in Akbar's revenue administration. The Dahsala system was associated with the assessment of land revenue.

Exam Point: Raja Todar Mal - Akbar's revenue administration.

8. Akbar's Navratnas

The famous nine personalities associated with Akbar's court included:

  • Birbal
  • Raja Todar Mal
  • Raja Man Singh
  • Abul Fazl
  • Faizi
  • Tansen
  • Abdul Rahim Khan-i-Khana
  • Fakir Aziao-Din
  • Mulla Do-Piyaza

9. Important Buildings

  • Fatehpur Sikri - developed as Akbar's capital.
  • Buland Darwaza - famous gateway at Fatehpur Sikri.
  • Akbar's Tomb - located at Sikandra near Agra.

10. Important Dates

  • 1542 - Birth of Akbar
  • 1556 - Akbar became emperor
  • 1556 - Second Battle of Panipat
  • 1563 - Pilgrimage tax abolished
  • 1564 - Jizya abolished
  • 1572 - Gujarat conquered
  • 1582 - Din-i-Ilahi introduced
  • 1605 - Death of Akbar
