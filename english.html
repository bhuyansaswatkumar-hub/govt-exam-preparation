1. Parts of Speech

There are eight major parts of speech:

1. Noun: Name of a person, place, thing or idea.
Example: Ram, Delhi, Book, Honesty
2. Pronoun: Used instead of a noun.
Example: He, She, It, They
3. Verb: Shows an action or state.
Example: Run, Eat, Write, Is
4. Adjective: Describes a noun or pronoun.
Example: Good, Beautiful, Tall
5. Adverb: Describes a verb, adjective or another adverb.
Example: Quickly, Very, Slowly
6. Preposition: Shows relationship between words.
Example: In, On, At, By, With
7. Conjunction: Joins words or sentences.
Example: And, But, Or, Because
8. Interjection: Expresses sudden emotion.
Example: Wow!, Alas!, Hurrah!

2. Tenses

Present Tense

Simple Present: I play cricket.

Present Continuous: I am playing cricket.

Present Perfect: I have played cricket.

Present Perfect Continuous: I have been playing cricket.

Past Tense

Simple Past: I played cricket.

Past Continuous: I was playing cricket.

Past Perfect: I had played cricket.

Past Perfect Continuous: I had been playing cricket.

Future Tense

Simple Future: I will play cricket.

Future Continuous: I will be playing cricket.

Future Perfect: I will have played cricket.

Future Perfect Continuous: I will have been playing cricket.

3. Articles

A is used before a consonant sound.

Example: a boy, a university

An is used before a vowel sound.

Example: an apple, an honest man

The is used for something specific.

Example: The Sun, The Ganga

4. Prepositions

In: He lives in Delhi.

On: The book is on the table.

At: He is at school.

By: The letter was written by Ram.

With: He went with his friend.

For: This gift is for you.

5. Subject-Verb Agreement

A singular subject generally takes a singular verb.

He plays cricket.
She goes to school.

A plural subject generally takes a plural verb.

They play cricket.
We go to school.

6. Active and Passive Voice

Active: Ram writes a letter.

Passive: A letter is written by Ram.

Active Voice focuses on the doer.
Passive Voice focuses on the action/object.

7. Direct and Indirect Speech

Direct: Ram said, "I am busy."

Indirect: Ram said that he was busy.

Common changes:
am → was
is → was
are → were
will → would
can → could

Hot — Cold

Big — Small

10. One Word Substitution

One who cannot read or write — Illiterate

One who loves mankind — Philanthropist

One who knows everything — Omniscient

One who speaks many languages — Polyglot

A person who writes poems — Poet

A place where books are kept — Library

A person who hates mankind — Misanthrope

A government by the people — Democracy

11. Idioms and Phrases

Break the ice: Start a conversation.

Once in a blue moon: Very rarely.

Piece of cake: Very easy.

Hit the nail on the head: Say exactly the right thing.

Spill the beans: Reveal a secret.

Under the weather: Feeling ill.

In hot water: In trouble.

Blessing in disguise: Something good that initially seems bad.

12. Common Error Rules

Incorrect: He don't like tea.
Correct: He doesn't like tea.
Incorrect: She is senior than me.
Correct: She is senior to me.
Incorrect: He is good in English.
Correct: He is good at English.
Incorrect: I prefer tea than coffee.
Correct: I prefer tea to coffee.

13. Sentence Improvement

Focus on:

  • Subject-verb agreement
  • Tenses
  • Articles
  • Prepositions
  • Pronouns
  • Conjunctions
  • Vocabulary

14. Cloze Test Tips

  • Read the complete passage first.
  • Understand the context.
  • Check grammar.
  • Check vocabulary.
  • Look at the words before and after the blank.
  • Eliminate clearly incorrect options.

15. Reading Comprehension Tips

  • Read the passage carefully.
  • Identify the main idea.
  • Do not assume information that is not given.
  • Answer according to the passage.
  • Use elimination when confused.
