1. India – Basic Facts

  • India is located in South Asia.
  • India is the 7th largest country in the world by area.
  • India is the most populous country in the world.
  • India covers about 3.28 million square kilometres.
  • The Tropic of Cancer passes through India.
  • India has a coastline of about 7,516 km including islands.
  • India's mainland extends from Jammu and Kashmir in the north to Kanyakumari in the south.
  • India has 28 states and 8 Union Territories.

2. Physical Divisions of India

The major physical divisions are:

  • The Himalayan Mountains
  • The Northern Plains
  • The Peninsular Plateau
  • The Indian Desert
  • The Coastal Plains
  • The Islands

Himalayas

  • The Himalayas are young fold mountains.
  • They form India's northern mountain boundary.
  • Major ranges include Himadri, Himachal and Shiwalik.
  • Mount Everest is the world's highest peak.

Northern Plains

  • Formed mainly by the Indus, Ganga and Brahmaputra river systems.
  • Very fertile and densely populated.
  • Important for agriculture.

3. Indian Rivers

Major Himalayan Rivers

  • Indus
  • Ganga
  • Brahmaputra

Important Peninsular Rivers

  • Godavari – largest peninsular river.
  • Krishna
  • Kaveri
  • Mahanadi
  • Narmada
  • Tapti

Important Facts

  • Ganga is one of India's most important rivers.
  • Yamuna is a major tributary of the Ganga.
  • Narmada flows westward into the Arabian Sea.
  • Godavari is often called the "Dakshin Ganga".

4. Indian Climate

  • India has mainly a tropical monsoon climate.
  • The southwest monsoon brings most of India's rainfall.
  • The monsoon generally reaches Kerala around June.
  • Western disturbances bring winter rainfall to north-western India.
  • Tamil Nadu receives significant rainfall from the northeast monsoon.

Seasons in India

  • Cold Weather Season – December to February
  • Hot Weather Season – March to May
  • Southwest Monsoon Season – June to September
  • Retreating/Northeast Monsoon Season – October to November

5. Soils of India

Major Soil Types

  • Alluvial Soil: Found mainly in northern plains; highly fertile.
  • Black Soil: Also called Regur soil; suitable for cotton.
  • Red Soil: Found in parts of southern and eastern India.
  • Laterite Soil: Common in areas of heavy rainfall.
  • Desert Soil: Found mainly in Rajasthan.
  • Mountain Soil: Found in Himalayan and mountainous regions.

6. Agriculture in India

Major Crops

  • Rice – major food crop; requires high temperature and rainfall.
  • Wheat – major winter/rabi crop.
  • Maize – important food and commercial crop.
  • Cotton – major fibre crop.
  • Jute – important fibre crop; mainly grown in West Bengal and nearby areas.
  • Tea – important plantation crop.
  • Coffee – mainly produced in southern India.
  • Sugarcane – important commercial crop.

Types of Cropping Seasons

  • Kharif: Sown with the monsoon; rice, maize and cotton.
  • Rabi: Sown in winter; wheat, gram and mustard.
  • Zaid: Short summer season; watermelon, cucumber and some vegetables.

7. Mineral Resources

  • Iron ore is an important mineral resource of India.
  • Odisha, Chhattisgarh and Karnataka are important iron ore producing regions.
  • Coal is an important source of energy.
  • Jharkhand, Odisha, Chhattisgarh and West Bengal have major coalfields.
  • Mica is found in several parts of eastern and southern India.
  • Bauxite is the main ore of aluminium.

8. Industries of India

Major Industries

  • Iron and Steel Industry
  • Cotton Textile Industry
  • Jute Industry
  • Sugar Industry
  • Cement Industry
  • Information Technology Industry
  • Petrochemical Industry

Important industrial centres developed near raw materials, power sources, markets, transport facilities and labour.

9. Important Lakes

  • Wular Lake – Jammu and Kashmir
  • Dal Lake – Jammu and Kashmir
  • Chilika Lake – Odisha
  • Sambhar Lake – Rajasthan
  • Loktak Lake – Manipur
  • Vembanad Lake – Kerala

Exam Fact: Chilika Lake is a famous brackish water lagoon in Odisha.

10. Important Waterfalls

  • Jog Falls – Karnataka
  • Shivanasamudra Falls – Karnataka
  • Dudhsagar Falls – Goa/Karnataka region
  • Hundru Falls – Jharkhand
  • Jog Falls is formed by the Sharavathi River.

11. Important Mountain Peaks

  • Mount Everest – world's highest peak.
  • Kangchenjunga – highest peak in India.
  • Nanda Devi – important Himalayan peak.
  • Anamudi – highest peak in South India.

12. Important Passes

  • Nathu La – Sikkim
  • Jelep La – Sikkim
  • Rohtang Pass – Himachal Pradesh
  • Shipki La – Himachal Pradesh
  • Zoji La – Jammu and Kashmir/Ladakh region
  • Banihal Pass – Jammu and Kashmir

13. Coastal Plains

Western Coastal Plain

  • Lies between the Western Ghats and Arabian Sea.
  • Generally narrower than the eastern coastal plain.

Eastern Coastal Plain

  • Lies between the Eastern Ghats and Bay of Bengal.
  • Generally wider than the western coastal plain.
  • Large river deltas are found here.

14. Indian Islands

  • Andaman and Nicobar Islands are located in the Bay of Bengal.
  • Lakshadweep Islands are located in the Arabian Sea.
  • Lakshadweep islands are mainly coral islands.
  • Indira Point is located in Great Nicobar Island.

15. Important Geography Facts for Exams

  • Capital of Odisha – Bhubaneswar.
  • Longest river in India – Ganga.
  • Largest peninsular river – Godavari.
  • Highest peak in India – Kangchenjunga.
  • Largest state by area – Rajasthan.
  • Smallest state by area – Goa.
  • Largest freshwater lake in India – Wular Lake.
  • Largest brackish water lagoon in India – Chilika Lake.
  • Thar Desert is mainly located in Rajasthan.
  • Western Ghats are also known as Sahyadri.
