❓ Current Affairs MCQs

1. From which date is the Bankers' Books Evidence Act, 2026 scheduled to come into force?

A. 1 September 2026
B. 1 October 2026
C. 1 November 2026
D. 1 December 2026

2. Which payment system was reported by PIB as remaining free for peer-to-peer transactions?

A. NEFT
B. RTGS
C. UPI
D. IMPS
Answer: C. UPI

3. Which Indian team was congratulated for winning the Asia Cup in the September 2026 update?

A. Indian Women's Cricket Team
B. Indian Men's Football Team
C. Indian Men's Hockey Team
D. Indian Women's Hockey Team

4. Which sector was highlighted in the PIB backgrounder titled "India Building Semiconductor Future"?

A. Textile sector
B. Semiconductor sector
C. Tourism sector
D. Fisheries sector
Answer: B. Semiconductor sector

5. What does PMAY-U primarily relate to?

A. Urban housing
B. Crop insurance
C. Railway recruitment
D. Digital payments
Answer: A. Urban housing
