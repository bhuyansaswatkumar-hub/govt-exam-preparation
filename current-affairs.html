🏦 Banking & Economy

  • Reserve Bank of India (RBI) is India's central bank.
  • Repo Rate is the rate at which RBI lends money to commercial banks.
  • Reverse Repo Rate is the rate at which RBI borrows money from commercial banks.
  • Inflation refers to a sustained rise in the general price level.
  • GDP stands for Gross Domestic Product.
  • SEBI regulates the securities market in India.
  • NABARD focuses on agriculture and rural development.

🏆 Awards & Honours

  • Bharat Ratna is India's highest civilian award.
  • Padma Vibhushan is one of India's major civilian honours.
  • Padma Bhushan and Padma Shri are important civilian awards.
  • Nobel Prize is awarded in several fields including Physics, Chemistry, Medicine, Literature and Peace.

🏅 Sports

🔬 Science & Technology
