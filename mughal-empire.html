📚 Mughal Empire - Quick Facts

  • Founder: Babur
  • Established: 1526
  • Dynasty: Mughal Dynasty
  • Important Emperors: Babur, Humayun, Akbar, Jahangir, Shah Jahan, Aurangzeb
  • First Battle of Panipat: 1526
  • Second Battle of Panipat: 1556
  • Last major Mughal ruler: Aurangzeb
  • Last Mughal Emperor: Bahadur Shah II

1. Babur

Zahir-ud-din Muhammad Babur founded the Mughal Empire in India. He defeated Ibrahim Lodi in the First Battle of Panipat in 1526.

  • Born: 1483
  • Founder of the Mughal Empire in India
  • Defeated Ibrahim Lodi in 1526
  • Defeated Rana Sanga in the Battle of Khanwa in 1527
  • Wrote his autobiography, Baburnama
Exam Point: First Battle of Panipat - 1526 - Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi.

2. Humayun

Humayun was the son of Babur. He succeeded Babur as Mughal emperor. He temporarily lost his empire to Sher Shah Suri but later regained Delhi.

  • Father: Babur
  • Son: Akbar
  • Defeated by Sher Shah Suri
  • Regained Delhi in 1555
  • Died in 1556

3. Akbar

Akbar became emperor in 1556 at a young age. He greatly expanded and strengthened the Mughal Empire.

  • Reign: 1556–1605
  • Second Battle of Panipat: 1556
  • Introduced the Mansabdari system
  • Abolished Jizya in 1564
  • Established Ibadat Khana at Fatehpur Sikri
  • Promoted Sulh-i-Kul
Read Complete Akbar Notes

4. Jahangir

Jahangir was the son of Akbar and succeeded him in 1605. His original name was Prince Salim.

  • Original name: Prince Salim
  • Reign: 1605–1627
  • Son of Akbar
  • Wife: Nur Jahan
  • Wrote his memoirs known as Tuzuk-i-Jahangiri
Exam Point: Jahangir is associated with the famous "Chain of Justice".

5. Shah Jahan

Shah Jahan is famous for Mughal architecture. His reign is often regarded as the golden age of Mughal architecture.

  • Reign: 1628–1658
  • Built the Taj Mahal
  • Built the Red Fort in Delhi
  • Built Jama Masjid in Delhi
  • Son of Jahangir
Exam Point: Taj Mahal was built by Shah Jahan in memory of Mumtaz Mahal.

6. Aurangzeb

Aurangzeb was one of the most powerful Mughal rulers. He expanded the empire to its greatest territorial extent.

  • Reign: 1658–1707
  • Also known as Alamgir
  • Son of Shah Jahan
  • Reimposed Jizya in 1679
  • Expanded Mughal control into large parts of the Deccan
  • Died in 1707

7. Important Mughal Battles

  • First Battle of Panipat - 1526: Babur defeated Ibrahim Lodi.
  • Battle of Khanwa - 1527: Babur defeated Rana Sanga.
  • Battle of Chanderi - 1528: Babur defeated Medini Rai.
  • Second Battle of Panipat - 1556: Akbar's forces defeated Hemu.
  • Battle of Haldighati - 1576: Mughal forces fought Maharana Pratap.

8. Mughal Administration

Mansabdari System

Akbar developed the Mansabdari system to organize government officials and military commanders according to ranks.

Revenue System

Raja Todar Mal played an important role in Akbar's revenue administration. The system of land revenue assessment became an important part of Mughal administration.

9. Mughal Architecture

  • Taj Mahal - Shah Jahan
  • Red Fort, Delhi - Shah Jahan
  • Jama Masjid, Delhi - Shah Jahan
  • Fatehpur Sikri - Akbar
  • Buland Darwaza - Akbar
  • Humayun's Tomb - built during the reign of Akbar

10. Important Mughal Books

  • Baburnama - Babur
  • Humayun-nama - Gulbadan Begum
  • Akbarnama - Abul Fazl
  • Ain-i-Akbari - Abul Fazl
  • Tuzuk-i-Jahangiri - Jahangir

11. Important Dates

  • 1526 - First Battle of Panipat
  • 1527 - Battle of Khanwa
  • 1555 - Humayun regained Delhi
  • 1556 - Akbar became emperor
  • 1556 - Second Battle of Panipat
  • 1605 - Jahangir became emperor
  • 1628 - Shah Jahan became emperor
  • 1658 - Aurangzeb became emperor
  • 1707 - Death of Aurangzeb
  • 1857 - End of Mughal rule after the Revolt of 1857
